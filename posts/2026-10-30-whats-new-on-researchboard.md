ResearchBoard grew a lot this month. Here is a quick tour of what is new — all free, all updated automatically.

## 🎓 US universities for Master's & PhD

A big map of **1,863 US universities that award master's degrees** (764 also award PhDs). Tap any university to see its **graduate application fee, tuition for international students, number of international students, top programs, TOEFL requirement and research output**, with buttons to apply. Under the map: the lowest-tuition research universities, universities with **no application fee**, and more.

👉 [US universities map](../us-universities/) · [Read the guide](us-universities-map-for-masters-and-phd.html)

## ✈️ Study Abroad

Four tabs in one place:

- **Scholarships** — fully funded and partial scholarships worldwide, each with its official website and deadline
- **Funded professors** — 10 countries where professors pay Master's and PhD students from their grants
- **US active grants** — professors with active NSF and NIH grants
- **Canada active grants** — professors with active NSERC and CIHR grants

👉 [Study Abroad](../study/) · [Read the guide](study-abroad-scholarships-and-funded-positions.html)

## 🔎 Find scientists — and your supervisor

Search active researchers worldwide by **name, research topic, country, field or university**, ranked by OpenAlex citations, h-index or i10-index. Every scientist has a profile page with their papers.

👉 [Find scientists](../scientists/) · [How to find a research supervisor in 5 minutes](find-a-research-supervisor.html)

## 🏛️ All universities

A searchable list of about **29,700 universities and research institutions** in every country, with papers, citations, h-index, open-access share and website. Each university has its own page with its top scientists. Every country page now shows its **top institutions** and its **top 5 most-cited scientists**.

👉 [Universities](../universities/) · [Countries](../country/)

## 📈 Research trends — Weekly pulse

Which countries, research fields and universities are **gaining or losing share** of world research this week compared with last week? The Weekly pulse shows the movers, and a ticker on the homepage shows them live.

👉 [Weekly pulse](../market/)

## 🧭 Research Compass

Which research field fits you? Answer **12 picture questions** in 2 minutes and get your research archetype — then see live data for your field.

👉 [Play Research Compass](../quiz/)

## A simpler menu

The menu is shorter now. **Research Map** opens the world map, and the new **Research Trends** menu holds the **Weekly pulse**, **Research fields** and **Journals**.
