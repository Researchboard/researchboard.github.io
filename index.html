          About the numbers

          • PubMed numbers come from NCBI E-utilities and count records added that day. Country counts search author affiliations, so a paper with authors in two countries counts for both.
          • Worldwide papers, countries, universities and citations come from OpenAlex. Many journals give only a month, which is stored as the 1st — expect spikes on the 1st of each month.
          • Each day shown is the last complete UTC day. The last 7 days are re-counted daily, so recent points move up as late papers arrive.
          • Citation totals of scientists update when OpenAlex refreshes them, which is not always daily; a gain can cover more than one day.
          Data: PubMed / NCBI · OpenAlex (CC0). Updated automatically every day.
