About the numbers

Papers are OpenAlex articles, reviews, preprints, letters, conference papers and book chapters, by publication date. PubMed counts records added that day. The Research Market strip shows what was added in the last 24 hours; everything else shows the latest fully counted day (about 2 days back), because journals report papers over several days.
